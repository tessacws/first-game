This policy explains what information the mobile game Pair Pop 3D (“the Game”), published by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] (“we”, “us”), collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or other contact details. Your game progress (level, stars, coins and power-ups) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device type, operating system, approximate location (derived from IP), and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google, not by us. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. On iOS, the Game asks for permission before your advertising identifier is used for tracking. You can change these choices at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads; iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking).

Rewarded ads and purchases

Watching an optional rewarded ad gives in-game rewards such as coins, extra time or power-ups. In-game coins have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged for money.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

Because game progress stays on your device, we do not store it on any server. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google’s policies.
